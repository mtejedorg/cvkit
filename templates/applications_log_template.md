# Applications log

One row per application folder in `_generated/`. The skill appends a row in Phase 7 and updates `sent` and `outcome` in Phase 9. Dates are YYYY-MM-DD.

- **fit:** `strong`, `workable` or `unlikely` (Phase 1 fit check).
- **score:** 0–100 from Phase 5, or blank if not scored. Gates must have passed.
- **sent:** the day the application went out; blank until the user confirms.
- **outcome:** `draft`, `sent`, `viewed`, `no-reply` (21+ days), `rejected`, `interview`, `offer`, `withdrawn`; add the date and the reason when the email gives one, e.g. `rejected 2026-10-06 (experience not closely aligned)`.
- **channel:** company site, LinkedIn Easy Apply, job board, recruiter, referral, direct email.
